not a superpower. not a disability.

tl;dr

adhd isn’t a tragedy or a cheat code. it’s a brain that can’t start without pressure and can’t stop once it does. the output is real and so is the bill. systems are how you pay it on purpose.

the two stories

the internet needs adhd to be one of two things. a tragedy, or a cheat code.

both stories are easier to tell than the real one, and both leave you with nothing to do on a tuesday.

the real one

it’s a brain that can’t start without pressure, and can’t stop once it does.

it forgets the thing it loves and locks onto the thing it doesn’t.

it sees the pattern faster than it can explain it, then forgets to send the email explaining it.

the bill

the output is real. so is the bill. late nights, dropped follow-ups, the thing you meant to do yesterday.

you can’t cancel the bill. you can choose to pay it on purpose instead of by surprise.

that’s all a system is: deciding in advance where the cost lands, so it doesn’t land on the thing that matters.

everything else here is one of those systems.

try this

pick one day from last week. write down every place the bill came due: something started late, something ran long, something dropped.

then paste it into claude or chatgpt with this prompt:

“here’s what went sideways in my day. don’t give advice yet. just show me the pattern: what time, what kind of task, what happened right before.”

the pattern is the first thing to design around.
