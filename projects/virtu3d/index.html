3d product development

virtu3d

at cleverviral · my part: strategy, targeting and copy.

$78.2kpipeline sourced
120qualified leads
9deals across 4 verticals

the situation

virtu3d lets fashion and product teams design and approve in 3d before any physical sample is made. the buyer is narrow (brands already working in 3d), senior, and talks differently in apparel, footwear, sports and jewelry.

what we did

  1. built the list only from brands with a real 3d signal: 3d designers, 3d-tagged product pages, open 3d roles.

  2. modeled each brand’s sample spend from its catalog and opened on the waste.

    ~2x reply

  3. ran separate campaigns per vertical, each in its own product-development language.

  4. triggered on open technical-designer roles, the exact job the product automates.

    ~2.5x reply

  5. wrote a separate recipe for browzwear customers, positioned as upstream, not a replacement.

an opener that worked

“saw you’re hiring a technical designer, virtu3d auto-generates the tech packs that role grinds out.”

“most outreach always comes across as very generic, very non-personal, when i read your emails, they are personal, they’re well done. i would respond to those emails.”
