try-before-you-buy for shopify plus

trynow

at cleverviral · my part: strategy, targeting and copy.

237qualified leads
62.5%from founders, ceos and vps
210kemails sent

the situation

trynow adds a try-before-you-buy checkout to shopify plus stores. only a founder or head of ecommerce can say yes, most had never considered the model, and the doubt changes by category: shade in beauty, fit in apparel, sizing in jewelry.

what we did

  1. opened on each brand’s own return policy. a strict policy is exactly the confidence gap trynow closes.

  2. put the volume where the doubt is physical, beauty and apparel, with a separate recipe per vertical.

    117 campaigns

  3. did the math in the email: an order-uplift estimate from the brand’s own public traffic.

  4. quoted their product reviews back to them.

    ~2x reply

  5. reached brands already visiting trynow’s site first, and reopened threads that had gone quiet.

    +77% and 12x the average positive-reply rate

an opener that worked

“roughly one in four of your recent reviews mentions sizing, which is the exact doubt trying at home removes.”

“you guys have been absolutely crushing it for us. you took cold outbound from a non-existent channel into a meaningful revenue driver in 6 weeks.”

benjamin davisfounder, trynow
