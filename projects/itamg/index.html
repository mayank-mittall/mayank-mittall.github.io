it asset disposition

itamg

at cleverviral · my part: strategy, targeting and copy.

0.1% → 22.9%positive reply rate
178qualified leads
69%from hands-on mid-level it

the situation

itamg pays companies for retired it equipment and wipes it securely. nobody thinks about disposal until a migration forces it, the buyer cares first about who handles their data, and default outbound lands on the c-suite, which is the wrong person here.

what we did

  1. led with security credibility before interest (gartner rating, r2 certification, 20+ years), with question-led openers for a technical buyer.

    0.1% to 22.9% over five months, holding near 20%

  2. timed sends to migration hires like cloud architects, since every migration creates a disposal event 3 to 6 months later.

  3. gave before asking: a fill-in-the-blanks data-sanitization policy for regulated industries.

    ~2x reply

  4. used dated triggers: windows 10 end of support, and announced mergers.

    ~2x reply

  5. split industries into four tracks, each on its own compliance frame (hipaa, soc 2, public-sector data).

an opener that worked

“most teams planned the buy side of the win10 retirement, not the disposal side.”

“shoutout to this entire team, you all are doing a great job and we have really enjoyed partnering. some big deals in the process from all of your work.”
